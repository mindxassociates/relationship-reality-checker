The cycle may repeat—and may change

1. Tension

Stress, monitoring, walking on eggshells, attempts to prevent an outburst.

2. Incident

Verbal, emotional, physical, sexual, digital, or financial harm.

3. Reconnection

Apologies, affection, promises, gifts, or pressure to forgive.

4. Calm

A quieter period that can restore hope before tension returns.

Not every abusive relationship follows four neat phases. The calm phase may shorten or disappear. Responsibility always belongs to the person choosing harmful behavior.

FOG detector

Fear, obligation, and guilt

Fear — “Something bad will happen”

Threats, intimidation, exposure, abandonment, retaliation, or “no one will believe you.” Ask: Am I choosing, or trying to prevent a consequence?

Obligation — “I owe them”

Money, gifts, time, sacrifice, or relationship roles are used as debts. Ask: Would a freely given choice require repayment through compliance?

Guilt — “I am bad if I say no”

Your limit is reframed as cruelty, selfishness, or betrayal. Ask: Can I care about their feelings without abandoning my boundary?

A quick reality test

Healthy influence allows questions, time, disagreement, and a genuine no. Coercion narrows your choices through fear or penalty.

Boundary reset

From escalation to a safer response

1

Notice and pause

Feel your feet. Take three slower breaths. “I’m going to pause so I can think clearly.”

2

State the limit

“I’m willing to talk when there is no yelling.” A boundary names what you will do.

3

Watch the response

Respect, curiosity, and repair are different from blame, ridicule, punishment, or escalation.

4

Choose the next safe step

Continue, take space, contact support, document safely, or leave. Co-regulation is only appropriate when both people can act safely.

Safety first

If you feel unsafe

Trust urgency. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services when safe to do so. Consider a trusted person, a safer room or exit, essential documents/medications, a code word, and a device the other person cannot access.
